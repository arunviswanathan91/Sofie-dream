## The Story

Sofie is a multi-talented handmade creator — she does custom embroidery, macramé, rope lamps, handmade clothing, accessories, and everything in between. Like so many artisans who turn their passion into a side business, she was juggling customer messages on Instagram, scribbled order notes, handwritten prices, and due dates kept in her head.

**Sofi Dream** was built specifically for her. For that kind of soul — the one who pours hours of skill and real craft love into something truly one-of-a-kind, then forgets to send the invoice. For the maker who has seven open orders across five different customers and can't remember which one is overdue. For every handmade creator who deserves a real studio management tool that feels as crafted and personal as the things they make.

No subscriptions. No mandatory cloud account. No complexity. Just your orders, your inventory, your invoices — all stored locally on your phone, always available, always yours.

## Why Sofi Dream is Different

Most business management apps are built for shops, restaurants, or e-commerce stores. Handmade creators don't work like that. Here's what makes Sofi Dream different:

- **Built for one-person artisan businesses.** No team management, no complex workflows — just you, your craft, and your customers.
- **Every invoice number is auto-generated.** Never wonder what to number your next invoice. It's tracked, it appears on PDFs and CSVs, and it's always there.
- **Pricing is informed by your real material costs.** The inventory engine knows what your materials cost and warns you if you're about to undersell a piece.
- **100% offline.** No Wi-Fi needed to log an order, mark something as shipped, or generate an invoice. Everything runs locally on your device.
- **It understands the lifecycle of a custom handmade order** — from "customer request" to "accepted" to "in progress" to "shipped" to "delivered" — not just "open" and "closed."

## Features

### Artisan Studio Dashboard
- Personalised greeting with time-of-day context ("Good morning, Sofie ☀️")
- **Circular progress ring** — shows the average completion % across all active orders. Tap it to update each order's progress right from the home screen
- **Live stats at a glance:** active orders, due this week, shipped count, and month-to-date revenue
- "Needs Attention" section that automatically surfaces urgent and overdue orders
- "In Progress" horizontal strip with live countdown timers per order
- Quick action pills: New Order, View Reports, Export PDF
- A different craft-focused inspirational quote every single day

### Order Management
- Create orders with **multiple line items** — each with a name, quantity, and per-unit price
- Full order lifecycle: `Request → Accepted → Shipped → Delivered`
- Per-order **completion percentage** — track exactly how far along each piece is (0–100%)
- Attach photos from your camera or gallery, plus a source/reference link
- Set due dates, craft category, and custom tags
- Internal notes field — private reminders that never appear on invoices
- Track payment status with optional payment notes

### Auto-Generated Invoice Numbers
Every accepted order gets its own invoice number — generated and stored automatically. No manual number tracking ever. Invoice numbers appear everywhere they should:
- The in-app invoice preview
- Exported PDF invoices
- Monthly PDF and CSV reports

### Customer Management
- Customers are built automatically from your orders — no separate data entry needed
- Customer profiles show total orders, total amount spent, and full order history
- Stores phone number and Instagram handle for quick contact
- Full customer detail page with all past orders

### Inventory & Pricing Engine
Know what your work actually costs — and never undersell again:
- Add **inventory products** (e.g. "Rope Lamp", "Macramé Wall Piece") with an emoji and category
- Break down each product's **materials** — e.g. "500g Natural Cotton Rope at €0.02/g"
- Set a **markup multiplier** — the app auto-calculates a suggested selling price
- When creating a new order: if the asking price is below the inventory suggested price, you see a warning. Simple and effective
- Set **restock reminders** per product (e.g. "remind me in 2 weeks")

### Reports & Export
- Revenue charts by day and by craft category (line chart + bar chart)
- Switchable periods: **Week / Month / Quarter / Year**
- Top customer, average order value, unpaid totals summary
- **Export as PDF** — a formatted monthly business report with invoice numbers, order details, and revenue breakdown
- **Export as CSV** — all orders in a spreadsheet-ready format with invoice #, tags, payment status, notes, and more

### Invoices
- Tap any order to preview and generate a clean, professional invoice
- Includes your business name, address, GST/tax number (optional), and tagline
- Line items table with quantities and per-unit pricing
- Invoice number always present and auto-assigned
- Share directly from the app as a PDF to WhatsApp, email, or anywhere

### Smart Notifications
- Due date alerts at configurable intervals (1 day, 3 days, 7 days before — your choice)
- Daily digest every morning with a summary of what's active
- Weekly summary on a day of your choosing
- Shipping reminder X days after accepting an order
- Payment reminder X days after delivery
- All individually toggleable

### Five Craft-Inspired Themes
Each theme applies across the entire app instantly — no restart needed:

| Theme | Feel |
|-------|------|
| **Warm Cream** | Cozy ivory default — the artisan atelier look |
| **Dark Walnut** | Deep rich brown for late-night crafting sessions |
| **Soft Sage** | Calm, earthy green — grounded and natural |
| **Lavender** | Gentle purple — soft, elegant, creative |
| **System Default** | Follows your device's dark/light mode |

### Data & Backup
- **All data is stored 100% locally on your phone** — no account needed, no internet required
- Export a full backup as a `.json` file and save it anywhere (Google Drive, iCloud, USB, email to yourself)
- Restore from a backup file at any time
- Optional Google Drive and OneDrive sync available

### Business Profile
- Set your business name, tagline, address, and GST/tax number
- Everything you enter appears on your generated invoices
- Default currency selector: EUR, GBP, USD, CHF, INR, SEK, PLN
- Custom craft categories with emoji and colour coding
